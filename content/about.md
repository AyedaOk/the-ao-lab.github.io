## Photography on Linux

[@AyedaOkambawa](https://www.youtube.com/@AyedaOkambawa) is a photography channel for people who want to use Linux and free and open-source tools in their photography workflow.

Topics range from HDR and focus stacking, geotagging to custom Darktable Lua scripts and experimental AI denoising. Some videos are careful tutorials, while others are projects where I document what works, what does not, and what I learn along the way.

This website adds concise notes, commands, links, and follow-up information to those videos.
