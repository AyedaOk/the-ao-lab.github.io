## Photography on Linux

The AO Lab is a small photography channel about taking, organizing, and processing photographs with Linux and free and open-source software.

This site complements the videos with concise notes, commands, links, and practical references for tools such as Darktable, RawTherapee, ART, and other Linux-friendly photography software.

The goal is simple: make useful photography workflows easier to understand and repeat.
